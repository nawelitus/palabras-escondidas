# Spanish word list: licence and credits

`words_es.txt` is a derived work. It was generated by `tools/build_dictionary.py`
from the Spanish Hunspell dictionaries (`es_ES` and `es_AR`) of the RLA-ES project,
as distributed in https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries (directory `es/`).

The original dictionaries are tri-licensed (GPL v3+, LGPL v3+, MPL 1.1+). This
derived list is used and redistributed under the **Mozilla Public License 1.1**.
The license text is available at https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/1.1/.

Modifications: affixes expanded into full word forms, accents removed (the letter
n-tilde is kept), proper nouns and acronyms removed, words limited to 3-16 letters,
`es_ES` and `es_AR` merged and deduplicated.

Credit: RLA-ES (Recursos Lingüísticos Abiertos del Español), https://forja.rediris.es/projects/rla-es/
